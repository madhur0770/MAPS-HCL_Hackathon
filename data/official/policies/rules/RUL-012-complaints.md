# Complaints and Grievances

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines complaint categories, response targets and mandatory human handoff for Ujjwal Rail.

## 2. Scope

It applies to booking, refund, service, luggage, accessibility, privacy, safety and staff-conduct complaints. It does not allow the assistant to decide liability or promise a remedy.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Complaint categories

Complaints are classified as booking or payment, refund dispute, train disruption, luggage or pet issue, accessibility, staff conduct, privacy or security, safety or harassment, and other service quality. The record must include a trace ID, authenticated passenger ID, relevant booking reference when authorised, summary, timestamps and evidence locations.

### 3.2 Booking and refund disputes

A duplicate debit, missing booking, wrong status, incorrect deduction, missing operator-cancellation refund or refund delayed beyond the published initiation period requires a human finance or booking handoff. The assistant may show the calculation and cited rule but cannot alter the amount.

### 3.3 Safety and harassment

An ongoing threat, harassment report, medical emergency, unattended hazardous item or violence concern is urgent. The assistant must prioritise immediate human safety escalation, provide the fictional emergency channel configured by the system and avoid asking unnecessary questions. It must not investigate or promise an outcome.

### 3.4 Privacy and security

Suspected identity exposure, unauthorised booking access, leaked credential or request for another passenger's data is referred to the privacy or security desk. Responses and handoff bundles must redact full identifiers, payment data and secrets. The assistant must not confirm whether another person's booking exists.

### 3.5 Handoff and response targets

Safety and harassment cases are handed off immediately with a target acknowledgement of 15 minutes. Privacy or security cases target one hour. Active-travel booking issues target two hours. Refund and payment disputes target one business day. Other service complaints target two business days. These are acknowledgement targets, not resolution promises.

### 3.6 Resolution restrictions

Only authorised staff may approve compensation, waive a policy, restore inventory, change promotion order or decide misconduct. The assistant may create a complete context packet and state the target acknowledgement time. It must say that the outcome depends on review.

## 4. Exceptions

If authentication is unavailable, accept a minimal safety report without exposing booking data and send it for human verification. Repeated or abusive messages do not cancel a genuine safety escalation. Conflicting evidence must be preserved and referred, not resolved by the assistant.
