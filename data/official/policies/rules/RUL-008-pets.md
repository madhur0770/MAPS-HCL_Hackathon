# Pet Travel Rules

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines safe travel for permitted animals on Ujjwal Rail.

## 2. Scope

It applies to domestic dogs, cats and small caged birds. Livestock, reptiles, wild animals and hazardous species are not accepted as passenger pets.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Eligible classes and registration

Pets may travel only with a CNF passenger in FP or AC2. Registration must be completed at least 24 hours before origin departure and remains subject to space and safety approval. RAC and WL passengers cannot register a pet. One registered pet is permitted per booking.

### 3.2 Documentation

The passenger must provide a current fictional veterinary fitness certificate issued within ten days before travel and any route-specific permit recorded by Ujjwal Rail. The assistant records only verification status and must not store the full certificate in chat logs.

### 3.3 Charges and refunds

The pet charge is INR 600 plus INR 20 per started kilogram of animal and carrier. The weight is rounded upward to the next whole kilogram. Passenger-requested cancellation more than 24 hours before departure refunds 80% of the charge; at 24 hours or less it is non-refundable. Operator cancellation returns the full charge.

### 3.4 Safety restrictions

Except for trained assistance animals, a pet must remain in a secure, ventilated carrier in its assigned area. The passenger is responsible for food, water and sanitation. An aggressive, visibly ill, unregistered or unsafe animal may be refused after staff review. Pets may not occupy emergency exits or food-service areas.

### 3.5 Assistance animals

A trained assistance animal accompanying a passenger with an accessibility need may travel in any class that can safely accommodate it, without the pet charge or carrier requirement. Advance notice is requested but lack of notice alone does not authorise automatic refusal. Staff resolve safety conflicts.

## 4. Exceptions

The assistant cannot certify animal fitness or promise acceptance. A documentation dispute, route restriction, welfare concern or conflict with another passenger requires human review.
