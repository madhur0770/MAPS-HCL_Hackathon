# Passenger Concessions

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines fictional Ujjwal Rail concession categories, discounts and verification requirements.

## 2. Scope

It applies per eligible traveller on normal-quota base fare. It does not apply to Tatkal inventory, taxes, fees, supplements, luggage or pet charges.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Categories and discounts

| Category | Base-fare discount | Eligible classes |
|---|---:|---|
| Student Mobility | 40% | AC3, SL, CC |
| Senior Wellbeing | 25% | AC2, AC3, EC, SL, CC |
| Accessible Travel | 50% | FP, AC2, AC3, EC, SL, CC |
| Service Family | 20% | AC2, AC3, EC, SL, CC |

### 3.2 Eligibility

Each category is available only when the booking system records a current fictional Ujjwal Rail eligibility credential for that traveller. One concession may be selected per traveller. Discounts do not stack and cannot be transferred.

### 3.3 Verification

The traveller must present the credential category used at booking and identity evidence under RUL-006 Section 3.3. The assistant may explain requirements but cannot approve eligibility. Only the credential type, verification result and last four identifier characters may be retained.

### 3.4 Exclusions

Concessions do not apply to Tatkal quota, Tatkal supplements, service fees, taxes, excess luggage, pet charges or onboard purchases. Student Mobility and Service Family are unavailable in FP. A concession cannot be added after payment; the booking must be cancelled and rebooked, subject to inventory and RUL-003.

### 3.5 Refund treatment

The discounted base fare actually paid is the base fare for RUL-003 percentage deductions. The class fixed minimum still applies. The system must never calculate a refund using the undiscounted fare. Operator cancellation returns the discounted fare and all other eligible components under RUL-011.

## 4. Exceptions

A missing, expired or disputed credential requires human review and does not automatically prove ineligibility. Staff decide travel consequences. The assistant must not promise approval, continued inventory or a particular refund.
