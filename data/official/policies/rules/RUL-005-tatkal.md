# Tatkal Booking Rules

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines Ujjwal Rail's fictional short-notice Tatkal quota, supplements and refund restrictions.

## 2. Scope

It applies only when a passenger explicitly selects Tatkal inventory. It does not convert a normal booking into Tatkal after payment.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Eligibility

Tatkal is available to authenticated passengers for FP, AC2, AC3, EC, SL and CC when a train has a published Tatkal quota. One booking may contain no more than four travellers on the same train, date, route and class. Concessions do not apply.

### 3.2 Opening and cutoff

Tatkal opens at 10:00 Asia/Kolkata one calendar day before scheduled origin departure and closes exactly two hours before origin departure. A request before 10:00 is rejected. A request accepted exactly at the two-hour cutoff is rejected. Inventory is allocated only after payment is recorded.

### 3.3 Supplements

| Class | FP | AC2 | AC3 | EC | SL | CC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tatkal supplement per traveller | INR 500 | INR 400 | INR 300 | INR 350 | INR 180 | INR 150 |

The supplement is added after base fare and is not included in percentage cancellation calculations.

### 3.4 Cancellation and refund

A voluntarily cancelled CNF Tatkal traveller receives no base-fare or Tatkal-supplement refund; unused refundable taxes may be returned. Before departure, Tatkal RAC and WL travellers use the INR 40 and INR 20 deductions in RUL-003 Section 3.7, but the Tatkal supplement remains non-refundable. If an RAC or WL traveller becomes CNF before cancellation is accepted, the CNF Tatkal rule applies.

### 3.5 Restrictions

Tatkal inventory cannot be combined with a concession, transferred, moved to a different train or converted to normal quota. A boarding-station change may still be requested under RUL-010 if all its conditions are met.

## 4. Exceptions

When Ujjwal Rail cancels the train or a delay qualifies under RUL-011, base fare, Tatkal supplement, service fee and refundable taxes are returned. A duplicate charge or status mismatch requires human review.
