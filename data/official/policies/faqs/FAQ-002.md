# Which Travel Classes Are Available

## 1. Passenger Question

Which classes can I book on Ujjwal Rail?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Ujjwal Rail supports FP First Premium, AC2 Air Comfort 2 Tier, AC3 Air Comfort 3 Tier, EC Executive Chair, SL Sleeper and CC City Chair. FP, AC2, AC3 and SL use berth-style accommodation; EC and CC are reserved-seat classes. A particular train may offer only some of these classes.
