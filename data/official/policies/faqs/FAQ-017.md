# Refund Processing Time

## 1. Passenger Question

How long does a refund take?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

After a successful cancellation, card and wallet refunds are initiated within five business days. An approved cash-counter refund is available within two business days. These are Ujjwal Rail processing targets; later bank settlement time is outside the promise. A refund missing beyond the applicable initiation period should be handed to the finance or booking desk.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-003 Section 3.9 and RUL-012 Sections 3.2 and 3.5.
