# Original AC2 and AC3 Tatkal Supplements

## 1. Passenger Question

When does Tatkal open, and what supplement applies in AC2 and AC3?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Tatkal opens at 10:00 Asia/Kolkata one calendar day before origin departure and closes exactly two hours before origin departure. The supplement is INR 400 per AC2 traveller and INR 300 per AC3 traveller. A request accepted exactly at the two-hour cutoff is rejected.

## 3. Original Policy Reference

RUL-005 Sections 3.2 and 3.3, as effective on 2026-11-18.
