# Passenger Concession Discounts

## 1. Passenger Question

Which concessions does Ujjwal Rail offer?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

The fictional concessions are Student Mobility at 40% in AC3, SL and CC; Senior Wellbeing at 25% in AC2, AC3, EC, SL and CC; Accessible Travel at 50% in all six classes; and Service Family at 20% in AC2, AC3, EC, SL and CC. Only one concession may be used per traveller. Concessions apply to normal-quota base fare, not Tatkal, fees, taxes or add-ons, and require recorded eligibility verification.

RUL-009 Sections 3.1 to 3.5.
