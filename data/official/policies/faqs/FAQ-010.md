# Travelling with a Pet

## 1. Passenger Question

Can I take my pet on Ujjwal Rail?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Domestic dogs, cats and small caged birds may be registered when you have a CNF booking in FP or AC2. Registration closes 24 hours before origin departure and is subject to safety approval and space. The charge is INR 600 plus INR 20 per started kilogram of the animal and carrier. A current fictional veterinary fitness certificate is required. Trained assistance animals follow the separate exception in RUL-008 Section 3.5.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-008 Sections 2 and 3.1 to 3.5.
