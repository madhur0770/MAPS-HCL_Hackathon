# Submitting and Escalating a Complaint

## 1. Passenger Question

How will my complaint be handled, especially if it is urgent?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

The assistant records a traceable summary and sends the matter to the appropriate human desk. Safety or harassment reports are handed off immediately with a 15-minute acknowledgement target; privacy or security cases target one hour; active-travel booking issues two hours; refund disputes one business day; and other service complaints two business days. These are acknowledgement targets, not promised resolutions. An immediate safety handoff must not wait for complete booking or identity details.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-012 Sections 3.1 to 3.6 and CIR-006 Sections 3 to 6.
