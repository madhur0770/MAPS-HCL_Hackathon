# AC2 Ticket Radd Karne Par Katauti

## 1. Yatri ka Prashn

Agar main confirmed AC2 ticket 48 ghante se pehle radd karun, to kitni katauti hogi?

## 2. Helpdesk Uttar

Agar cancellation ke samay prasthan mein **48 ghante se adhik** baaki hain, to 2026-11-15 se lagu AC2 fixed charge INR 260 prati yatri katega. Theek 48 ghante baaki hone par agla band lagu hota hai: base fare ka 25% ya INR 260, jo adhik ho. Percentage discounted base fare par nikala jata hai. Service fee wapas nahi hoti aur final refund kabhi negative nahi ho sakta.

## 3. Adhikarik Niti Sandarbh

RUL-003 Sections 3.2 se 3.6 aur CIR-001 Sections 3 aur 4.

## 4. English Summary

For more than 48 hours remaining, the current AC2 fixed charge is INR 260. At exactly 48 hours, the deduction is the greater of 25% of discounted base fare or INR 260.
