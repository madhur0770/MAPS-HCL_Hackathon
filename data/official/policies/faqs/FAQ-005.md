# Can an RAC Passenger Travel

## 1. Passenger Question

Can I board if my status is RAC?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Yes. RAC permits travel with a shared-seat allocation, but it does not guarantee a full berth. You may be promoted to CNF if inventory is released, following the original booking timestamp and system sequence. The assistant cannot promise promotion or change your queue position.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-004 Sections 3.1, 3.3 and 3.6; CIR-003 clarifies the transaction timing for inventory release without changing these rights.
