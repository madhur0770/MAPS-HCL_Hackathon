# Boarding Change Deadline and Confirmation Clarification

## 1. Purpose

This circular clarifies which departure timestamp controls the six-hour boarding-change deadline and how a confirmation in progress is handled.

## 2. Reference to Existing Rule

RUL-010 Section 3.2 requires confirmation more than six hours before scheduled origin departure and rejects confirmation at exactly six hours. RUL-010 Section 3.5 requires a separate confirmation and verified record update.

## 3. Clarification

The controlling timestamp is the official scheduled origin departure stored in the timetable version attached to the booking. A forecast operational delay does not extend the deadline. If Ujjwal Rail formally reschedules the service and attaches the revised official schedule to the booking before confirmation, the revised scheduled origin departure controls.

The separate confirmation must be received and accepted while more than six hours remain. Starting a chat, receiving a preview or pressing Confirm before the deadline does not qualify if the action is accepted at or after the deadline. An idempotent retry of an already successful action returns the existing result and is not a second change.

## 4. Effective Date and Applicability

This clarification applies to boarding-change confirmations accepted on or after 2026-11-13 00:00 Asia/Kolkata. It does not reopen a previously rejected request and does not alter the six-hour numerical deadline.

## 5. Examples

If origin departure remains scheduled for 18:00, acceptance must occur before 12:00; exactly 12:00 is too late. A forecast delay to 20:00 does not change that result. If the official schedule attached to the booking is formally revised to 20:00 before action acceptance, the deadline becomes before 14:00.

## 6. Unchanged Provisions

Only one change is permitted, all active travellers move together, the new station must be later than the original boarding station and before the destination, and successful read-back verification remains mandatory.
