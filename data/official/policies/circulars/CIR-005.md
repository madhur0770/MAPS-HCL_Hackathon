# Future Revision to Excess Luggage Rate

## 1. Purpose

This circular announces a future change to the rate for registered luggage above the free class allowance.

## 2. Reference to Existing Rule

RUL-007 Section 3.2 currently charges INR 30 per started kilogram above the class allowance and permits no more than 30 kg of excess passenger luggage.

## 3. Amendment Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Original rule | RUL-007 |
| Original section | 3.2 |
| Setting key | `excess_luggage_rate_per_started_kg` |
| Previous value | INR 30 |
| Revised value | INR 35 |
| Effective date | 2027-01-01 |
| Applicability | Excess-luggage registrations accepted on or after 2027-01-01 00:00 Asia/Kolkata |

The charge remains `started excess kilograms multiplied by the effective rate`. A fraction of a kilogram continues to round upward to the next whole kilogram before multiplication.

## 4. Effective Date and Applicability

Before 2027-01-01 00:00 Asia/Kolkata, RUL-007 Section 3.2 continues to apply at INR 30. On or after that instant, the rate is INR 35. Booking creation date and journey date do not select the rate; the accepted excess-luggage registration timestamp does. A completed pre-effective registration is not repriced.

## 5. Examples

For 2.2 kg above the allowance, the system charges three started kilograms. A registration accepted on 2026-12-31 costs INR 90. The same excess accepted on 2027-01-01 costs INR 105.

## 6. Unchanged Provisions

Free allowances by class, the 30 kg excess cap, restricted-item rules, registration deadline, cancellation refund percentage and operator-cancellation treatment remain unchanged.
