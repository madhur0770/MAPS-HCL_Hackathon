# Luggage Rules

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines free allowances, excess charges, restrictions and handling of passenger luggage.

## 2. Scope

It applies per CNF or RAC traveller. Pet containers are governed by RUL-008 and do not count as ordinary luggage.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Free allowance by class

| Class | FP | AC2 | AC3 | EC | SL | CC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Free allowance | 70 kg | 50 kg | 40 kg | 35 kg | 30 kg | 25 kg |

Allowances are per traveller and cannot be pooled across unrelated booking references.

### 3.2 Excess luggage

Up to 30 kg above the class allowance may be registered, subject to space. The charge is INR 30 per started kilogram above the allowance. A fraction of a kilogram is rounded upward to one kilogram. Luggage over the 30 kg excess cap requires cargo handling and cannot be accepted as passenger luggage.

### 3.3 Restricted items

Explosives, flammable fuel, corrosive substances, unsecured weapons, illegal goods, leaking containers and items that block exits are prohibited. Batteries and medical equipment must be safely packed. The assistant must refer uncertain or hazardous items to authorised staff rather than declare them safe.

### 3.4 Registration, cancellation and refunds

Excess luggage must be registered no later than 60 minutes before origin departure. A passenger cancellation made more than four hours before departure refunds 80% of the excess-luggage charge; at four hours or less, it is non-refundable. Operator cancellation returns the full recorded charge under RUL-011 Section 3.1.

### 3.5 Loss, damage and complaints

Report missing or visibly damaged registered luggage within six hours of arrival. Record the booking reference, luggage receipt and description without storing unnecessary personal data. Compensation is decided by staff and must not be promised by the assistant.

## 4. Exceptions

Mobility aids and essential medical devices do not consume the free allowance when safely stowed. Oversized, hazardous or disputed items require human review. Ujjwal Rail may refuse an item even when its weight is within the allowance if it cannot be safely carried.
