# Operator Cancellations and Train Delays

## 1. Purpose

This rule defines refunds when Ujjwal Rail cancels a train or expects a significant departure delay.

## 2. Scope

It applies to operator-caused train-level disruption and is separate from voluntary cancellation under RUL-003.

## 3. Policy Conditions

### 3.1 Operator cancellation

When Ujjwal Rail marks a train cancelled for the passenger's booked route, every unused CNF, RAC and WL traveller receives a full refund without a voluntary-cancellation deduction. The refund includes base fare, refundable and non-refundable service fees, taxes, Tatkal supplement and operator-collected luggage or pet charges. Automatic processing should begin within 24 hours; no passenger cancellation is required.

### 3.2 Qualifying delay

A delay qualifies when the forecast or actual departure from the passenger's recorded boarding station is at least three hours later than scheduled. Exactly three hours qualifies. A passenger who has not begun travel may decline the journey before actual departure and claim the same full refund described in Section 3.1.

### 3.3 Non-qualifying or travelled delay

A delay under three hours does not create an automatic full-refund right. A passenger who travels after a qualifying delay cannot also receive the automatic unused-journey refund. Consequential loss, a missed separate booking, accommodation cost or dissatisfaction after travel requires human review and is not automatically payable.

### 3.4 Claims and processing

An operator-cancellation or qualifying-delay claim must be submitted no later than 72 hours after scheduled departure from the train's origin. The automatic cancellation refund does not require a claim, but a missing refund may be disputed within the same period. Card and wallet refunds are initiated within five business days; approved cash-counter refunds are available within two business days.

### 3.5 Evidence and distinction from voluntary cancellation

Eligibility must come from the train-status system and booking record, not an LLM estimate. If a passenger voluntarily cancels before an operator cancellation or qualifying delay is recorded, RUL-003 applies unless staff establish that the disruption already existed and approve reclassification.

## 4. Exceptions

A partially cancelled route, diversion that skips the boarding or destination station, conflicting train-status record, commenced journey or disputed cause requires human review. Safety evacuations are escalated immediately. The assistant may explain eligibility but cannot promise consequential compensation.
