# AC3 Tatkal Waitlist Refund Kaise Milega

## 1. Passenger Question

Meri AC3 Tatkal booking WL mein hai. Agar seat confirm nahi hoti, to cancellation aur refund kaise hoga?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Tatkal booking origin departure se ek calendar day pehle 10:00 baje open hoti hai aur exactly two hours pehle close hoti hai. 2026-11-20 se AC3 Tatkal supplement INR 330 hai. Agar WL traveller departure se pehle cancellation karta hai, to INR 20 status deduction lagta hai, lekin Tatkal supplement refundable nahi hota. Agar cancellation accept hone se pehle status CNF ho jaye, to confirmed Tatkal cancellation rule apply hoga: base fare aur Tatkal supplement refund nahi honge, par unused refundable taxes wapas mil sakte hain.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-005 Sections 3.2 to 3.4, RUL-003 Section 3.7 and CIR-002 Sections 3 and 4.

## 4. English Summary

The current AC3 Tatkal supplement is INR 330. A WL Tatkal cancellation before departure uses the INR 20 WL deduction, but the supplement remains non-refundable; promotion to CNF before accepted cancellation triggers the confirmed Tatkal rule.
