# Cancelling a Waitlisted Traveller

## 1. Passenger Question

What happens if I cancel a waitlisted traveller?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Before departure, Ujjwal Rail deducts INR 20 for a cancelled WL traveller and returns the remaining refundable amount. A traveller still on WL is automatically cancelled two hours before the train leaves its origin, with the same INR 20 deduction. If the traveller becomes CNF before cancellation is accepted, the CNF cancellation table applies instead.

RUL-003 Section 3.7 and RUL-004 Section 3.5.
