# How Early Can I Book a Ticket

## 1. Passenger Question

How early can I book a normal Ujjwal Rail ticket?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Normal booking opens at 08:00 Asia/Kolkata exactly 90 calendar days before the train leaves its origin. The origin departure date is day zero. Booking closes 20 minutes before origin departure, even if you board at a later station. Availability is not guaranteed when the window opens.
