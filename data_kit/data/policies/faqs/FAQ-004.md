# Confirmed AC2 Refund Calculation Example

## 1. Passenger Question

Can you show an example refund for an AC2 confirmed ticket cancelled more than 48 hours before departure?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Assume the discounted base fare actually paid is INR 1,000, refundable taxes are INR 50 and the non-refundable service fee is INR 30. At more than 48 hours, Band A uses the effective AC2 fixed charge of INR 260. The refund is `INR 1,000 - INR 260 + INR 50 = INR 790`. The INR 30 service fee is not returned. The calculation cannot produce a negative refund.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-003 Sections 3.2, 3.4 and 3.6, with CIR-001 Sections 3 and 4 effective from 2026-11-15.
