# Yatra ke Liye Pahchan Praman

## 1. Yatri ka Prashn

Yatra ke samay kaun sa pahchan praman sweekar hoga?

## 2. Helpdesk Uttar

Ek vayask yatri current government photo identity, educational-institution photo card, employer photo card ya Ujjwal Rail verified passenger credential mein se koi ek dikha sakta hai. Ek hi booking mein kam se kam ek vayask ke paas sweekarya original ya verifiable digital credential hona chahiye. Chat mein poora identity number ya document image na bhejen; system sirf category, issuer label aur identifier ke aakhri chaar characters rakhta hai.

## 3. Adhikarik Niti Sandarbh

RUL-006 Sections 3.1, 3.3 aur 3.6.

## 4. English Summary

Adults may use one approved identity category. At least one adult on the booking must present acceptable evidence, and full identifiers must not be exposed in chat or logs.
