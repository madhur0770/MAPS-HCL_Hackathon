# Original Fixed Cancellation Charges for AC2 and AC3

## 1. Passenger Question

What fixed cancellation charge applies to an AC2 or AC3 confirmed ticket?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

For a voluntary cancellation where the class fixed charge applies, the charge is INR 240 per AC2 traveller and INR 200 per AC3 traveller. In later time bands, Ujjwal Rail compares this fixed charge with the applicable percentage of base fare and uses the greater amount.

## 3. Original Policy Reference

RUL-003 Sections 3.3, 3.4 and 3.6, as effective on 2026-11-12.
