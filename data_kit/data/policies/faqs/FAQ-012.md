# Boarding Station Badalne ki Samay Seema

## 1. Yatri ka Prashn

Main apna boarding station kab tak badal sakta hoon?

## 2. Helpdesk Uttar

Booking dharak ek booking ka boarding station sirf ek baar badal sakta hai. Confirmation official origin departure se **chhah ghante se adhik** pehle accept honi chahiye; theek chhah ghante par request der se mani jayegi. Anumanit delay se samay seema nahi badhegi. Naya station usi route par original boarding station ke baad aur destination se pehle hona chahiye. Sirf preview dekhna kaafi nahi hai; alag confirmation aur verified update zaroori hai.

## 3. Adhikarik Niti Sandarbh

RUL-010 Sections 3.1 to 3.5 and CIR-004 Sections 3 to 5.

## 4. English Summary

One boarding-station change is allowed. It must be accepted more than six hours before the official scheduled origin departure and must select a later stop before the destination.
