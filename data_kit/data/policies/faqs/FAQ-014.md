# Refund for a Heavily Delayed Train

## 1. Passenger Question

Can I get a refund if my train is heavily delayed?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

If forecast or actual departure from your recorded boarding station is delayed by at least three hours, you may decline travel before actual departure and claim a full refund. Exactly three hours qualifies. A delay under three hours does not create that automatic right, and a passenger who travels cannot also claim the unused-journey refund. Claims must be submitted within 72 hours after scheduled origin departure.
