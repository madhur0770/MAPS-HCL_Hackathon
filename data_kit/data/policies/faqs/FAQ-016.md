# Mixed CNF RAC and WL Travellers

## 1. Passenger Question

What happens when people on one booking have CNF, RAC and WL statuses?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Each traveller keeps an individual status. CNF and RAC travellers may travel; a WL traveller may not board unless promoted. Cancelling or promoting one traveller does not copy that status to the others. Promotion follows the original successful booking timestamp and system sequence after released inventory is committed. No queue position or promotion can be promised.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-004 Sections 3.3 to 3.6 and CIR-003 Sections 3 to 6.
