# Free and Excess Luggage Allowance

## 1. Passenger Question

How much luggage can I carry, and what is the current excess rate?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

The free allowance per traveller is FP 70 kg, AC2 50 kg, AC3 40 kg, EC 35 kg, SL 30 kg and CC 25 kg. Up to 30 kg above the allowance may be registered, subject to space. As of 2026-11-21, the charge is INR 30 per started excess kilogram. CIR-005 changes that rate to INR 35 only for registrations accepted on or after 2027-01-01; it does not apply yet.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-007 Sections 3.1 and 3.2; CIR-005 Sections 3 and 4 for the future-effective rate.
