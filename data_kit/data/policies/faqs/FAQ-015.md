# Cancelling Only One Traveller

## 1. Passenger Question

Can I cancel one traveller without cancelling everyone on the booking?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Yes. Cancellation is evaluated per traveller using that traveller's status and fare components at the accepted cancellation time. Other travellers remain active. Shared fees are split equally, with any one-rupee remainder assigned to the final traveller. If the cancelled person was the lead contact, replacement contact details may be required.

## 3. Authoritative Policy Reference

RUL-003 Section 3.8 and RUL-004 Section 3.4.
