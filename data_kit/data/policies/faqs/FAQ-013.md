# Refund When Ujjwal Rail Cancels a Train

## 1. Passenger Question

What will be refunded if Ujjwal Rail cancels my train?

## 2. Helpdesk Answer

Every unused CNF, RAC and WL traveller receives a full refund without a voluntary-cancellation deduction. It includes base fare, service fees, taxes, Tatkal supplement and operator-collected luggage or pet charges. Automatic processing should begin within 24 hours, so you do not need to cancel the booking yourself. The assistant cannot promise the bank's settlement time.
